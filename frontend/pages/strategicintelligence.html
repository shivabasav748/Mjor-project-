Key Strategic Trade-offs

Illustrative · frontend-only
SpeedQuality
Leaning toward Quality. Trust is the product in elder care — reliability matters more than shipping fast. Recommended balance: favor quality, but timebox validation cycles.
GrowthEfficiency
Leaning toward Efficiency. Pre-validation, spend should stay lean. Recommended balance: prove retention before paying for growth.
BreadthFocus
Strongly Focus. One workflow, one segment, proven well, beats a broad feature set. Recommended balance: stay narrow until product-market fit is clear.
InnovationStability
Balanced, leaning Innovation. AI differentiation is the wedge, but reliability can't be sacrificed for it. Recommended balance: innovate on assistance, keep the core experience stable.
